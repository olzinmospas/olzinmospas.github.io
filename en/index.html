About me.

Full biography

Antonio Romeo Fernández (Burgos, 1984) began as a lyricist for the band Domador and went on to establish himself as a music critic in publications such as Diario del Altoaragón, Rockdelux and El Estado Mental.

He has also published several short stories and contributed to various collective works. He currently lives in Portugal, where he is working on a new novel.
