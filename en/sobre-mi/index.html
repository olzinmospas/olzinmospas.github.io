The biography.

Antonio Romeo Fernández (Burgos, Spain, 1984) began his career as a lyricist for the band Domador and went on to establish himself as a music critic in publications such as Diario del Altoaragón, Rockdelux and El Estado Mental.

He has also published several short stories and contributed to a number of collective works. He currently lives in Portugal, where he is working on a new novel.

Between 2011 and 2015 he wrote 135 music chronicles, reviews and anti-chronicles: urgent writing about what was playing, what was failing and what deserved to be told. That archive is preserved in full in the archive of this site (in Spanish).
